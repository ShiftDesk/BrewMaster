Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

No account needed No data on our servers No tracking or analytics No ads

01Introduction

BrewMaster is an iPhone app made by chmays.app. We care about your privacy. This policy explains what information the app uses and how it is handled.

02Data We Collect

We do not collect any personal data. Everything you create in BrewMaster, including journal entries, ratings, beans, flavor notes, photos, café visits, favorites and settings, is stored on your device and, if you use iCloud, in your own private iCloud account.

We do not run any servers for BrewMaster, and we have no access to your data.

03iCloud Sync

If you are signed in to iCloud, your journal and beans are synced through Apple's iCloud Key-Value Store. This data is private to your Apple ID and is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy. We cannot see it.

04Location

BrewMaster only asks for your location when you log a brew at a café and tap "Use my location". Your location is used once, on your device, to find cafés near you through Apple Maps, so you can pick the right one for your Café Map.

  • Location is only used while you are using the app ("When In Use"). It is never tracked in the background.
  • Only the location of the café you choose is saved with that journal entry, on your device and in your iCloud.
  • You can always search for a café by name instead, without giving location access.
  • You can turn location access off anytime in iPhone Settings.

Café searches are handled by Apple Maps and covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

05Camera

The Bean Scanner uses the camera to read the barcode on a coffee bag. The camera is only active while the scanner is open. No images are saved or sent anywhere. Only the barcode number is read.

06Photos

If you add a photo to a journal entry, it is saved in BrewMaster's private storage on your device. Photos are never uploaded to any server.

07Notifications

BrewMaster can send you a daily reminder with the Drink of the Day and a reminder before your coffee beans run out. All notifications are created on your device. No notification data is sent to any server. You can turn them off anytime in the app or in iPhone Settings.

08Home Screen Widget

The BrewMaster widget shows the Drink of the Day and your brewing streak. To do this, the app shares a small amount of data (your streak, number of brews and the drink list) with the widget through a private App Group on your device. This data never leaves your iPhone.

09Sharing

When you share a brew card, the image is created on your device and sent only where you choose through the iOS share sheet. We do not receive a copy.

10Purchases

BrewMaster Pro is a one-time in-app purchase handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see or store your payment details. Purchases are verified directly with Apple.

11Third-Party Services

BrewMaster does not use any third-party analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and we do not share or sell any data. The only outside services the app uses are Apple's own: iCloud, Apple Maps, the App Store and notifications.

12Deleting Your Data

You can delete any journal entry or bean bag inside the app. Deleting the app removes all data stored on your device. To remove synced data, you can manage BrewMaster's iCloud data in iPhone Settings under your Apple ID, then iCloud.

13Children's Privacy

BrewMaster is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any information from children.

14Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new date. If you keep using the app after a change, it means you accept the updated policy.

15Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Email us at support@chmays.app.
